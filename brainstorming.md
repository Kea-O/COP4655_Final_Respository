# Initial brainstorming ideas
- [X] **Recipe app for cooking**. Users can favorite and download the best recipes. APIs can be used to generate recipes users can look through or search for.
- [X] **Drawing notes app**. Users can type down notes or draw with their fingers/pens. Similar to OneNote, their pages can be saved and stored in collections.
- [ ] **Movie and TV app**. Users can see what's new and trending acoss the globe, and what theaters are showing them. Can feature multiple APIs.
- [ ] **Interview/flashcard app**. Users can either create or have questions generated for them on a wide range of subjects to keep their mind sharp. Can use some APIs
- [ ] **Fun fact app**. An app that shows the user fun facts; can be specific (such as astronomy - "what's new in space today?") or general.
- [X] **Some sort of game-based app** where Users can level-up/collect/do challenges to keep their characters happy/strong.

## Evaluating Top 3 Ideas 
- [X] **Recipe app** has the most potential. I can envision what I'd want for this app easily, and ways too expand upon the initial idea. I can see myself working well on this because I can picture what I want out of this app in my head.
- [ ] I feel that the **notes app** has the second most potential. I use such apps in my daily life, so I know what I'd like to change or edit to fit my style. I can also see this as a good learning opportunity because of its general complexity. However, I think that it might be a bit too ambitious for the amount of time left in the semester.
- [ ] The **game-based app** is third because while I have some interest in the subject, I can't picture what type of app I'd want to create as strongly as the top 2 options. It could be a good learning experience, especially since the visual-focused design of such apps would lead me to exprimenting more with dynamically changing code and systems.

## Choose the Best App
Ultimately, I believe that the **recipe app** is the best fit for me. The simplicity is enough to prevent me from being overwhelmed while also allowing me avenues to expand upon the idea and learn. I feel that I have a strong vision for this app, too, so I'll be able to stay focused on what is good for development. It'll also just be generally useful, since I'm a college student who may need to cook alone.
